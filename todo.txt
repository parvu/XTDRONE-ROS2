CH15
 - problem:
 why get_local_pose shows noting
- solution:
get_local_pose publishes poses on ROS topics; it doesn’t print each pose to the terminal. Keep it running, open another terminal, and run
ros2 topic echo /iris_0/mavros/vision_pose/pose
o see the values. That’s how the node is written in get_local_pose.py.
